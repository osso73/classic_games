# About

## Background

The games were originally written one at a time with plain Kivy, and later brought
together under a single KivyMD shell, **MDClassics**. The standalone game folders
and their historical releases are kept in the repository for reference, but the
combined app in `source/MDclassic_games` is the focus of current development. Its
release history is in the
[combined-app changelog](changelogs/classic_games_changelog.md).
