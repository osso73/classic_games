The current combined app (MDClassics) has its own changelog at
[`source/MDclassic_games/CHANGELOG.md`](source/MDclassic_games/CHANGELOG.md). A copy
is published with the documentation at
[classic_games_changelog](https://osso73.github.io/classic_games/changelogs/classic_games_changelog/).

The games were originally released separately, and their historical changelogs are
kept together in the [`changelogs`](https://github.com/osso73/classic_games/tree/main/docs/changelogs)
folder, as part of the documentation of the program.
