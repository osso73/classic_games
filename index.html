Classic games

A series of classic games built with Kivy and KivyMD, combined into a single app, MDClassics, with seven games:

  • Pong
  • Ahorcado (hangman)
  • Memory
  • 15 puzzle
  • 2048
  • Buscaminas (minesweeper)
  • Snake

How to play each game, its controls and its settings are documented in the combined app help. To install and run the app on your computer, see Getting started.
