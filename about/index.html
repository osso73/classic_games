About

Background

The games were originally written one at a time with plain Kivy, and later brought together under a single KivyMD shell, MDClassics. The standalone game folders and their historical releases are kept in the repository for reference, but the combined app in source/MDclassic_games is the focus of current development. Its release history is in the combined-app changelog.

License

The project code is released under the MIT license. Some bundled images and sounds are third-party assets that may not be used commercially. Only use the project commercially if you replace them with license-free material.
